Chicken Popcorn

Description: This is a simple chicken popcorn recipe.View Image

Ingredients:

  • 3 cups flour
  • 2 teaspoons garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 2 cups oil, or as needed
  • 3 skinless, boneless chicken breasts, cubed

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oil in a deep fryer or large pot to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a large bowl, combine the flour, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
  3. Beat the eggs and lemon juice together, then add to the flour mixture.
  4. Stir until a thick batter forms.
  5. Dredge the chicken pieces in the batter, then fry until golden brown and cooked through.
