Chicken Popcorn

This is a simple chicken popcorn recipe.View Image

Popcorn Chicken

Ingredients:

  • 3 cups flour
  • 2 teaspoons garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 2 cups oil, or as needed
  • 3 skinless, boneless chicken breasts, cubed
