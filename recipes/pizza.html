Pizza

Description: This is a pizza recipe.View Image

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp yeast
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt to taste

Instructions:

  1. In a large bowl, combine the flour, yeast, and salt.
  2. Add water and olive oil, and mix until a dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 5-10 minutes.
  4. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover, and let it rise for 1-2 hours.
  5. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C).
  6. Roll out the dough to your desired thickness and place it on a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  7. Add your favorite toppings and bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown.
