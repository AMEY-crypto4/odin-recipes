You can use either a pizza stone or a pan to bake the pizza. “It was golden and crispy on the outside and so soft on the inside.” —Allrecipes member Ginny Eckert Keep Screen Awake Ingredients 1/2x 1x 2x Original recipe (1X) yields 15 servings 1 ½ cups warm water (110 degrees F/45 degrees C) 2 ¼ teaspoons active dry yeast ½ teaspoon brown sugar 2 tablespoons olive oil 1 teaspoon salt 3 ⅓ cups all-purpose flour, divided Directions Stir together warm water, yeast, and brown sugar in a large mixing bowl; let sit for 10 minutes. Stir oil and salt into yeast mixture. Mix in 2 1/2 cups flour until incorporated. Turn dough out onto a clean, floured surface. Knead dough, adding remaining flour, a little at a time, until dough is no longer sticky. Place dough into an oiled bowl. Overhead shot of uncooked pizza dough in a glass bowl Dotdash Meredith Food Studios Cover with a towel and let rise until doubled in size, about 1 hour. Overhead shot of pizza dough in a bowl that has doubled in size Dotdash Meredith Food Studios Punch down dough and form it into a tight ball. Allow dough to relax for 1 minute before rolling out. Overhead shot of punched down pizza dough in a glass bowl Dotdash Meredith Food Studios Overhead shot of pizza dough on a floured, marble surface with a wooden rolling pin Dotdash Meredith Food Studios Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F (220 degrees C). If baking dough on a pizza stone, place toppings on dough and bake immediately. If baking dough on a pan, lightly oil the pan and let dough rise for 15 to 20 minutes before topping and baking it.
