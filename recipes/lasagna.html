Reviewers love using cream cheese instead of ricotta for a rich, creamy texture. As a review from Allrecipes member ADLUV notes, “It is a nice change from regular lasagna.” Keep Screen Awake Ingredients 1/2x 1x 2x Original recipe (1X) yields 6 servings 6 uncooked lasagna noodles 3 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese, divided 1 (8 ounce) package cream cheese, softened ¼ cup hot water 1 cube chicken bouillon 1 (26 ounce) jar spaghetti sauce Directions Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Ingredients for creamy chicken lasagna arranged on a countertop including chicken breasts tomato sauce lasagna sheets and cheese Allrecipes / Julia Hartbeck Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook lasagna noodles in boiling water, stirring occasionally, until tender yet firm to the bite, about 8 minutes. Drain, rinse with cold water, and set aside. Pasta sheets arranged on a plate Allrecipes / Julia Hartbeck Meanwhile, place chicken in a large saucepan with enough water to cover; bring to a boil. Cook until chicken is no longer pink and the juices run clear, about 20 minutes. Remove chicken from the saucepan and shred. Shredded chicken on a cutting board with a fork and knife placed next to it Allrecipes / Julia Hartbeck Combine shredded chicken, 1 cup mozzarella cheese, and cream cheese in a large bowl. Dissolve hot water and bouillon in a liquid measure; pour over chicken mixture and stir until well combined. Shredded chicken and other ingredients mixed in a clear bowl with a spoon Allrecipes / Julia Hartbeck Spread 1/3 of the spaghetti sauce in the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish. Cover with 1/2 of the chicken mixture and top with 3 lasagna noodles; repeat. Top with remaining sauce and sprinkle with remaining 1 cup mozzarella cheese. A casserole dish with unbaked lasagna topped with cheese and tomato sauce Allrecipes / Julia Hartbeck Bake in the preheated oven for 45 minutes. Creamy chicken lasagna served in a baking dish Allrecipes / Julia Hartbeck Serve and enjoy!
