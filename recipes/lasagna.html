Lasagna

Description: This is a lasagna recipe.View Image

Ingredients:

  • 1 package lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup parmesan cheese
  • 2 jars marinara sauce

Instructions:

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef until browned. Drain excess fat.
  4. Add marinara sauce to the cooked beef and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a baking dish, spread a layer of meat sauce, followed by a layer of noodles, ricotta cheese, and mozzarella cheese. Repeat layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with a layer of meat sauce and topping with parmesan cheese.
  6. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 25 minutes or until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  7. Let the lasagna cool for 10-15 minutes before serving.
