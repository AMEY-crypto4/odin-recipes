What to Serve with Beef Burgers Here are some easy serving suggestions that will turn this basic burger into a satisfying meal—whether you’re hosting a backyard cookout, planning a casual weeknight dinner, or feeding a hungry crowd: Homemade Crispy Seasoned French Fries Creamy Coleslaw Grilled Bacon-Wrapped Corn on the Cob How to Store and Reheat Beef Burgers Cooked beef burgers can be safely refrigerated for three to four days. Make sure to promptly chill after cooking in an airtight container to lengthen shelf life. To reheat, warm burgers gently to keep them juicy. Heat in a skillet over medium-low heat, covered, until warmed through, about 3 to 5 minutes per side. You can also reheat in a 350 degrees F (175 degrees C) oven, loosely wrapped in foil, for about 10 minutes or until heated through. For a quick option, microwave on medium power in 30-second intervals, covered with a damp paper towel to prevent drying. Burgers should be reheated until an instant-read thermometer inserted into the center reads at least 160 degrees F (70 degrees C). Can I Freeze Basic Beef Burgers? Yes, you can freeze cooked burger patties for up to four months. Wrap the burgers in foil individually to discourage sticking before labeling and placing them in a freezer-safe bag.
