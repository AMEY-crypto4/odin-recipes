Burger

Description: This is a burger recipe.View Image

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 bun
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Cheese

Instructions:

  1. Preheat a grill or skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Form the ground beef into a patty and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Cook the patty for 3-4 minutes on each side, or until it reaches your desired level of doneness.
  4. Toast the bun on the grill or skillet for 1-2 minutes.
  5. Assemble the burger by placing the cooked patty on the bottom bun, followed by lettuce, tomato, cheese, and the top bun.
